About me

I'm Stepan Diadorov — online I go by InsaneQA. I make Polemicon on my own: the code, the rules and every character's lines.

For years I wanted to make games. I learned Unity, even worked as a Unity Developer at Belka Games, and kept piling up ideas — but only one of them ever made it to a release, on Google Play. AI is what finally helped me carry the work through to the end.

Like everyone, I can't stand AI slop. So I hold myself to a hard rule: if a game isn't unique — the first of its kind — I won't make it.

I live in Łódź. Away from the keyboard I cook (I'm a big fan of eating, too), play computer games and chess.
